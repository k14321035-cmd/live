Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Last updated: November 2026 • Published by Tambanesu

Zero Tolerance Notice: Tambanesu maintains an absolute, uncompromising zero-tolerance policy toward any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We strictly ban and criminally report any user or content attempting to exploit, harm, or abuse children.

Tambanesu (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a private community application operated by Code Tutorium. This policy sets forth our mandatory safety standards, community rules, content screening practices, reporting mechanisms, and enforcement procedures designed to protect children and prevent Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable international law.

🚫 1. Strictly Prohibited Content and Conduct

We explicitly prohibit any user from uploading, transmitting, generating, soliciting, storing, promoting, or linking to content or behavior involving child sexual exploitation or abuse. Prohibited actions include, but are not limited to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual depiction (including photographs, videos, audio, digital illustrations, or synthetic/AI-generated media) of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct or depicted in a sexualized manner.
  • Child Grooming and Solicitation: Any attempt to establish an emotional connection with a minor to lower their inhibitions for sexual exploitation, solicit explicit communications, or arrange physical or digital sexual encounters.
  • Sextortion and Blackmail: Threatening to release intimate images, videos, or personal information of minors for extortion, coercion, or harassment.
  • Facilitation or Promotion of Abuse: Any posts, comments, direct messages, links, or groups promoting, enabling, advocating, or trading child sexual abuse content or child sexual exploitation.
  • Commercial Exploitation: Child trafficking, child sex tourism advertisement, or monetization linked to minors' sexual exploitation.

🛡️ 2. Preventive Safeguards & Content Moderation

To uphold these standards and maintain a safe platform, Tambanesu applies multiple layers of technical and human moderation:

  • Proactive Review & Filtering: Automated keyword detection and media scanning are deployed across feeds, comments, posts, and public profile assets to intercept prohibited terms and suspicious patterns.
  • Trained Human Review: Any content flagged by automated safeguards or community members is routed to expedited human review for rapid resolution.
  • Restricted Interactions: Private community architecture with strict moderation, department verification, and rate limits to block malicious actors from spamming or harvesting user contacts.

🚨 3. Reporting Channels & Immediate Escalation

We empower our community, guardians, and the public to report any suspected child abuse, grooming, or exploitation immediately. All child safety reports are prioritized with top urgency.

How to Report Violations:

  1. In-App Flagging: Tap the ⋮ (More Options) button on any post, reel, comment, or profile → Select Report → Choose Child Safety / CSAE. This immediately flags the item and removes it from your view pending moderation.
  2. Dedicated Child Safety Email: Email our safety desk directly at learncording@gmail.com (or via Contact Us — Code Tutorium) with the subject line URGENT: CHILD SAFETY REPORT. Include user handles, timestamps, links, or screenshots if available.

⚡ 4. Enforcement and Penalties

Upon identifying or confirming any violation involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, we take immediate, non-negotiable enforcement action:

  • Permanent Account Termination: The perpetrator's account is instantly and permanently revoked without warning or possibility of appeal.
  • Immediate Content Deletion: All associated media, posts, comments, and profile data are removed from public access and securely preserved solely for law enforcement handover.
  • Hardware & Identity Banning: Device identifiers, IP addresses, authentication tokens, and registered credentials are permanently blacklisted to prevent re-registration.

⚖️ 5. Law Enforcement Cooperation & NCMEC Reporting

In adherence to mandatory legal responsibilities and child protection standards:

  • We report any confirmed or suspected Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via their CyberTipline (www.cybertipline.org) and relevant statutory clearinghouses.
  • We proactively refer cases involving child safety risks to relevant national and local law enforcement agencies.
  • We preserve forensic records, logs, and account metadata in accordance with legal obligations to assist official criminal investigations and prosecutions.

🧸 6. Age Requirements & Minor Safeguarding

Tambanesu is designed for college community members aged [16 / 18] and over. We do not knowingly allow access to young children. If we discover that an account has been registered by an underage user or that a minor's information has been provided without verified authority, we will immediately delete the account and associated records.
