Effective Date: 07-06-2026

Our fundamental principle is privacy-first. Python Tutor is designed so that we do not manually collect, request, or store your personal identification data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising IDs (like Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA).

These third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies. We strongly encourage you to review their respective privacy practices.
