Privacy Policy for Tambanesu

Last updated: NOV 2026

Tambanesu (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a private community app for the students and staff of [COLLEGE FULL NAME], operated by Code Tutorium. You can contact us at Contact Us — Code Tutorium. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

🛡️ 1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, display name, and password. The password is handled by our authentication provider, and we never see it in plain text.
  • Profile information: the bio, department and profile picture you choose to add.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, reels, photos, videos, likes, saved items, and your fantasy game squad and team name.
  • Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, crash and performance data, and basic interaction data.
  • Advertising identifier: the Google Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, we store a token so we can send you alerts.
  • On-device storage: settings and cached data stored on your phone to keep you signed in and make the app faster.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment information.

🎯 2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and provide the app's features, including the college feed, reels, sports news, season updates, stats and the fantasy game.
  • To show your public content to other users of the app.
  • To send notifications about new posts, live streams and highlights, if you have allowed them.
  • To show ads that help keep the app running.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

📢 3. Third-party services

We use these providers to run the app. They process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase, for authentication, database and file storage.
  • Google AdMob, for advertising (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Google Play services and Firebase Cloud Messaging, for app delivery and notifications.

AdMob may use your advertising ID to show personalised or non-personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings under Google → Ads.

🤝 4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed above, to operate the app;
  • when required by law or to respond to valid legal requests;
  • to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the app.

Posts, comments and reels you publish may be visible to other Tambanesu users.

🗑️ 5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your own posts at any time. To delete your account and associated data, visit Contact Us — Code Tutorium or use the deletion option in the app [if available]. You can also use [ACCOUNT DELETION URL]. We will delete your data within [30] days, except where we must keep some information to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.
